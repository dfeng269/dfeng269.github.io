## Colorectal cancer and the tumor microenvironment

My current research examines the molecular and cellular mechanisms of colorectal cancer using multi-omics approaches. Areas described in my CV include macrophage complement factor D (CFD) deficiency, cancer-associated fibroblast states, extracellular matrix remodeling, and responses to immune checkpoint blockade.

## Single-cell RNA sequencing and spatial omics

I analyze single-cell transcriptomic and spatial omics datasets using R, Python and Linux-based computational workflows. These approaches help characterize cellular heterogeneity and investigate candidate biological mechanisms in cancer and other systems.

## Neurobiology and circadian rhythms

My doctoral and postdoctoral research focused on zebrafish visual and circadian biology, including Per2/Per1b-related visual phenotypes, pineal neuron heterogeneity, neuronal projections and in vivo imaging of cellular circadian oscillators.

## Methods and skills

- **Computational:** R, Python, Linux, single-cell transcriptomics, spatial omics, public dataset mining and multi-omics analysis.
- **Experimental:** molecular biology, genome editing, cell culture, animal experiments, behavioral assays, staining, microscopy and image analysis.
